Wapee kya hai

Ek page ka khulasa: product kis ke liye hai, kis ke liye nahin, aur kis tarah bana hai.

Wapee ek international appointment-booking SaaS hai. Koi bhi business jo "time slot" bechta hai (salon, barber, clinic, dental, physio, pet grooming, consultant, car service) Wapee par apne services, staff aur timings daalta hai, aur customers wapee.online/<slug>/book par khud booking kar lete hain. Business ko dashboard milta hai (Today, Calendar, Clients, Invoices, Reports), ek single-page website, aur zaroorat ke hisaab se add-ons.

Kis ke liye hai

  • Chhote aur darmiyane appointment businesses: 1 akela owner (Solo) se le kar kai branches (Business) tak.
  • Har mulk: koi bhi country, currency aur time zone (280 countries, 162 currencies, 418 time zones).
  • Jo flat price chahte hain, per-seat billing nahin, aur marketplace commission nahin.
  • Jinhein clinic jaisi depth chahiye: forms, consent, photos, packages (add-ons).

Kis ke liye nahin (abhi)

  • Jinhein customer se online payment / deposit lena hai (built nahin, scope se bahar).
  • Recurring ya ek booking mein kai services (scope se bahar).
  • Waitlist, custom domain, drag-and-drop calendar, marketing email campaigns, dark mode in app.
  • Jo app store wali native app aaj hi chahte hain (PWA live hai, store apps baad mein).
  • Jinhein SMS reminders zaroori hain (SMS launch par off hai).

Key principles

  • Generic first: ek hi engine, industry ka farq sirf settings aur custom fields se. Har industry ka alag code nahin.
  • International: country, currency, time zone, tax aur address branch ke paas hain, business ke paas nahin. Reports kabhi do currencies ko jama nahin karte.
  • Simple UX: smart defaults, ek line ki explanation, live preview, advanced settings band (collapsed).
  • Safe data: double booking database khud rokta hai; invoices ek baar issue ho kar badal nahin sakte; har table par RLS.

Tech ek paragraph mein

Ek hi Expo (React Native) codebase se web, PWA, Android aur iOS bante hain. Abhi web + PWA launch ho raha hai, store apps baad mein. Sab kuch ek domain par hai: https://wapee.online (landing page, dashboard, admin aur business pages; koi app. subdomain nahin). Website Vercel par host hogi. Backend Supabase hai: Postgres database, login (Auth), file storage aur Edge Functions (chhote server programs). Email Resend se jaata hai, browser push notifications Web Push se. SMS ke liye Twilio ka code hai magar band hai. Brand naam sirf ek file (src/config/brand.ts) mein likha hai, taake rename asaan rahe.

Add-ons

Add-on = extra module jo plan ke upar on kiya jata hai. On karna sirf super admin ke haath mein hai (owner "Contact us" dabata hai). Trial mein sab add-ons on hote hain.

Off hone par kya hota hai (sab add-ons ka common rule): menu, screens, buttons aur role editor ki permission rows gayab ho jaati hain, aur database us module ke har action ko feature_unavailable keh kar refuse karta hai. Data delete nahin hota, bas chhup jata hai; dobara on karne par wapas aa jata hai.
Packages & Sessions$9/month · $90/year · har plan parLive
Kya karta hai
Business "package" banata hai (jaise 10 sessions + 1 bonus, price, expiry, kin services par chale). Client ko invoice line ke taur par bikta hai, aur har visit par appointment se ek session kaat'ta hai.
Kis ko chahiye
Laser clinics, physio, massage, gym-type sessions, prepaid bundles.
Misaal
"Laser 6 sessions $300": invoice issue hua, client ke profile par 6 balance. Har visit par appointment drawer mein "Use a session".
Kahan
Menu Packages (definitions), invoice editor mein "Add package", client profile par Packages section, appointment drawer mein Package sessions block.
Rules
  • Package invoice issue hone par bikta hai (unpaid ho tab bhi sessions chalte hain). Invoice par client hona zaroori.
  • Expiry branch time zone ke din ke hisaab se.
  • Galat deduction: banane wala 10 minute tak Undo; baad mein sirf reverse permission wala (reason ke saath). Har cheez nayi row, purani kabhi delete nahin.
  • Poora credit note package cancel karta hai, magar sirf jab koi session use na hua ho.
  • Staff apni appointments par session kaat sakta hai.
  • Customer khud cancel kare (manage link ya portal se, visit se pehle) to kata hua session khud wapas ho jata hai (nayi reversal row, audit ke saath). Staff dashboard se cancel kare to session khud wapas nahin hota; staff haath se reverse karta hai.
Missing
Packages report, Today/Calendar par package badge.

Products (naam, SKU, unit, price, stock, low-stock level). Invoice par product line bikti hai to stock khud kam hota hai. Har stock change ek permanent record.
Kis ko chahiye
Salons jo shampoo/products bechte hain, clinics jo creams bechte hain.
Misaal
Shampoo stock 5, alert level 2. Invoice par 3 bike, stock 2, Today par "Low stock" badge aur Manager ko push + email alert.
Kahan

  • Products har branch ke apne. Branch ke beech transfer nahin.
Partial
Low-stock push aur email bana hai magar asal mein kabhi deliver test nahin hua. Barcode, suppliers, purchase orders nahin.

Appointments: statuses aur actionsAppointment detail drawerLive
Statuses
pending (approval ka intezar) · confirmed · completed · no_show · cancelled.
Actions
  • Approve (pending se confirmed): appointments.approve.
  • Complete: review link dialog khulta hai, review email queue hoti hai.
  • No-show: hamesha haath se; khud-ba-khud nahin hota. Appointment ka waqt shuru hone se pehle no-show nahin ho sakta (button nazar nahin aata, server bhi rokta hai). Waqt se pehle "Completed" ho sakta hai, magar warning aati hai.
  • Cancel: appointments.cancel (Staff ke paas nahin). Cancelled hamesha ke liye final.
  • Reopen (completed/no-show se confirmed): sirf Owner ya Manager, aur sirf jab invoice na bana ho.
  • Drawer mein client ka phone/email (Call, WhatsApp, Email, Copy) agar clients.view_contact ho; messages ka status aur fail hone par "Try again".
  • Booking form answers: customer ne booking form ke sawalon ke jo jawab diye, wohi sawal ke us waqt ke label ke saath. Sensitive jawab sirf clients.view_sensitive walon ko (lock ke saath "Sensitive"); Staff sirf apni appointments ke.

Data safety

  • RLS: har table par database khud check karta hai kaun kya dekh sakta hai.
  • Double booking database ke rule se band.
  • Paise ke records kabhi edit/delete nahin.
  • Audit log: role changes, paise, deletes, admin access.
  • Files private, 5 min ke links.

Rate limits + bot protection

  • Booking: ek IP se 10 per ghanta; ek phone se 5 per din; ek business par 30/ghanta, 120/din.
  • Slots / booking page / website: 60 per minute per IP.
  • Review submit 10/ghanta; portal login 3/ghanta per email.
  • Chhupa honeypot field, 3 second se tez form refuse.
  • CAPTCHA nahin.

Backups

  • Aaj: Supabase free plan, koi backup nahin.
  • Plan: Pro par roz backup; PITR baad mein; storage files ki weekly copy alag (backup files cover nahin karta).
  • Monitoring / uptime alerts abhi nahin.
